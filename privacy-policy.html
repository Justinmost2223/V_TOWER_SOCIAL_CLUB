2. Data We Process

  • Identity and contact data: full name, personal email, university email and campus.
  • Account data: your password is stored only as a secure hash (bcrypt) and can never be read by us.
  • Programme data: your referral code, referral count and the rewards you unlock.
  • Consent records: date, version of the text, your choices and the browser identifier at the time of acceptance.
  • Google sign-in data: if you choose "Continue with Google", only your name and email address.

3. Purposes and Legal Bases

  • Verifying active student status and managing your account, passes, guest lists and VIP reservations (free or promotional): performance of the service you request, Art. 6.1(b) GDPR.
  • Managing the referral programme and rewards: Art. 6.1(b) GDPR.
  • Commercial communications (invitations, promotions, event information): your explicit and optional consent, Art. 6.1(a) GDPR and Art. 21 LSSI-CE. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Security, fraud and abuse prevention (for example fake or duplicate accounts): legitimate interest, Art. 6.1(f) GDPR.
  • Compliance with legal obligations and defence of legal claims: Art. 6.1(c) and 6.1(f) GDPR.

Providing the data marked as required is necessary to create your account. Marketing consent is optional and does not affect your access. We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects on you.

4. Recipients and Processors

  • Supabase Inc. — database, authentication and hosting of user data (data processor).
  • Vercel Inc. — website hosting (data processor); it may process technical data such as your IP address.
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Partner venues — only where necessary to confirm your access, we share your name and pass details with the venue concerned.
  • Public authorities — where required by law.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Infrastructure, Security and International Transfers

User data is stored on Supabase infrastructure in servers located within the European Economic Area (European Union). We apply encryption in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest, secure password hashing (bcrypt), strict per-user access rules at database level (Row Level Security) and the principle of least privilege, in line with Article 32 GDPR. Where a provider belongs to a group established outside the EEA (for example in the United States), transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. Marketing data is used until you withdraw your consent. After you delete your account or withdraw consent, your data is blocked (Art. 32 LOPDGDD): it remains inaccessible and is kept solely to address possible legal, commercial or administrative liabilities for a legal period of 6 years from your last interaction or deregistration, in line with Article 30 of the Spanish Commercial Code and applicable limitation periods. After that period it is permanently deleted. Consent records are kept for the same period as proof of compliance.

7. Your Rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), restriction of processing, portability and objection, and withdraw any consent given, by emailing vtowersocialclub@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy Rights". We may ask you to verify your identity and will reply within one month. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD), www.aepd.es.

8. Commercial Communications (Direct Marketing)

By voluntarily ticking the optional marketing checkbox, you give explicit consent, in accordance with Article 21.1 of the LSSI-CE and Article 6.1(a) of the GDPR, to receive at your personal email address invitations, access promotions, free passes and VIP reservation offers managed by V Tower Social Club.

Every promotional email will include a direct, automatic unsubscribe link at the bottom of the message, allowing you to withdraw your consent at any time without losing your user account.

9. Sign-in with Google

If you sign in with "Continue with Google", we receive from your Google account only the name and email address needed to create and manage your account. We do not access any other Google account data. You must still complete your student details and accept our conditions.

10. Biometric Data

V Tower Social Club does not collect, process or store any biometric data, and does not receive any information from the access systems of any university.

11. Cookies, Local Storage and Third-Party Resources

We use only strictly necessary browser storage: your login session, your referral code and a flag that prevents repeating reward notifications. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. To display this website, your browser loads fonts and scripts from third-party providers (such as Google Fonts, jsDelivr and the Tailwind CDN), which may receive technical data such as your IP address.

12. Minors

Our services are intended exclusively for persons aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors and will delete it if we become aware of it.

13. Record of Consent

For accountability purposes, we keep a record of your acceptance of this Policy and the Terms & Conditions and of your choice regarding commercial communications (date, version of the text and status), linked to your account.

14. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect legal or operational changes. The current version and its date are always shown on this page, and material changes will be notified to registered users.
